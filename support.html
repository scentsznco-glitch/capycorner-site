How to play

Place exactly one capybara in every row, every column and every color region. Capybaras can't touch each other, not even diagonally. Tap a cell to mark it with an ✕, double-tap to place a capy, and swipe to mark several cells at once. You can replay the tutorial anytime from Settings.

FAQ

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the app erases it.

Sound doesn't play? Check the Ring/Silent switch and that Sound is on in Settings.

I'm out of hints. Hints and capy reveals are saved between levels. You earn more by playing (a free hint every 5 levels, Daily Puzzle and streak rewards), or you can watch a short, optional video for +1.

Does the game have ads? Yes, a few short ads served by Google AdMob. See our Privacy Policy for details and your choices.
